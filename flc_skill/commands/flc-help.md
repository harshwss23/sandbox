The contributor wants to know what to do. Answer from the `flc_workflow` skill
and from the guides in `~/flc/contributor_instructions/`.

Start by checking where they actually are:

```bash
python3 ~/flc/bin/status.py
```

Then give them, briefly:

1. what this project is building and why -- a research task that catches a model
   inventing facts, not just failing to find them
2. the step they are on and what it needs
3. the full list of commands, in order, so they can see the shape of it

If the question was more specific than "what do I do", answer that instead. Do
not recite the whole workflow at someone who asked how to write a rubric --
point them at `~/flc/contributor_instructions/05-rubrics.md` and answer the
actual question.
